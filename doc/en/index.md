# QMapShack documentation

With QMapShack you plan your hikes and rides and archive those you have done. Plan a track on the map and use it
on your GPS device. Back home, read the recording into QMapShack, refine it and save it in your database.

Work with all kinds of maps side by side, together with elevation data and routing data. That makes QMapShack the
perfect tool to explore the world.
