# Quick start

The quick start takes you from the first start of QMapShack to a track on your GPS device. The images on all pages
show the same area, the Tannheimer Tal in Austria. Each page also works on its own; read in order, they follow how
you set up QMapShack.


- [The main window](quickstart-main-window.md): the panels around the map and how to arrange them; the home folder for your data.
- [Turning on maps](quickstart-maps.md): the built-in online maps, stacking them and making one see-through.
- [Turning on a Garmin map](quickstart-vector-map.md): Garmin vector maps, their level of detail and their look.
- [Zooming and panning](quickstart-zoom-pan.md): moving around the map and the zoom range a map is shown in.
- [Elevation data and hillshading](quickstart-dem.md): adding elevation data (a digital elevation model, DEM), the
    elevation under the mouse pointer and shaded terrain.
- [Loading a GPX file](quickstart-load-file.md): loading a file of tracks and waypoints, looking at them and finding
    them on the map.
- [Setting up routing](quickstart-routing.md): a router that draws tracks along paths and roads; Routino and
    BRouter.
- [Drawing a track](quickstart-draw-track.md): a track of your own, along paths, along another track or
    in a straight line; correcting and saving it.
- [Adding waypoints](quickstart-waypoints.md): a waypoint of your own, its name and symbol.
- [Databases](quickstart-database.md): projects in one file, sorted into folders.
- [Saving your work](quickstart-save.md): keeping your projects as a file or in a database.
- [Using a GPS device](quickstart-device.md): putting tracks and waypoints on a Garmin device and reading
    its recordings.
