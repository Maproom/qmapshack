# Quick start

The quick start takes you from the first start of QMapShack to a track on your GPS device. The images on all pages
show the same area, the Tannheimer Tal in Austria. Each page also stands on its own. Read in sequence, they guide you through setting up QMapShack.


- [The main window](quickstart-main-window.md): the panels around the map and how to arrange them. The home folder for your data.
- [Enabling maps](quickstart-maps.md): the built-in online maps, stacking them and making one transparent.
- [Enabling a Garmin map](quickstart-vector-map.md): Garmin vector maps, their level of detail and how they look.
- [Zooming and panning](quickstart-zoom-pan.md): navigating the map and adjusting its zoom range.
- [Elevation data and hillshading](quickstart-dem.md): adding elevation data (a digital elevation model, DEM), the
    elevation under the mouse pointer and terrain shading.
- [Loading a GPX file](quickstart-load-file.md): loading tracks and waypoints from a file, viewing them, and locating them on the map.
- [Setting up routing](quickstart-routing.md): a router that creates tracks along paths and roads. Routino and
    BRouter.
- [Drawing a track](quickstart-draw-track.md): creating your own track along paths, following an existing track, or drawing it in a straight line. Editing and saving it.
- [Adding waypoints](quickstart-waypoints.md): creating your own waypoint and choosing its name and symbol.
- [Databases](quickstart-database.md): organizing projects in a single file, grouped into folders.
- [Saving your work](quickstart-save.md): storing your projects in a file or a database.
- [Using a GPS device](quickstart-device.md): transferring tracks and waypoints to a Garmin device and importing its recorded tracks.
